# Recording session guide (~20 minutes)

Setup: `python tools/record_webcam.py --minutes 20`. Face the docking-station webcam
straight on, face filling about the middle third of the frame, light from the front
(a window or lamp behind the camera), no hand or mic in front of your mouth.
Glasses are fine. Don't worry about mistakes - keep talking.

## 1. Read aloud, normal voice (5 min)
Sentences chosen to cover every English sound, including lots of lip closures
(p, b, m), lip rounding (oo, w), and teeth-on-lip (f, v).

- The big brown puppy jumped over my muddy boots.
- Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers.
- We were away a while, wondering where Will went.
- Five fluffy vans view the very fine valley.
- She sells seashells by the seashore, she thinks.
- Thirty thousand thoughtful theaters thanked them.
- Could you choose cheap juice or chilled chocolate?
- Rory's lawn rake rarely rolls really well.
- My mom made maple muffins Monday morning.
- Yesterday the young yak yawned at you.
- Go get the good goat, Greg, quickly.
- How hungry is the huge hippo, honestly?
- Oh, no, I don't know, you go, though.
- Each eagle eats eighteen green beans.
- The pool is cool, but the moon is too blue.

## 2. Same sentences, with feeling (5 min)
Read 5–6 of them again: very happy, annoyed, sad, surprised, whispered, shouted
(not too loud for the mic), and with a big smile the whole time.

## 3. Talk freely (8 min)
Explain something you know well (synths, a game, a class) as if on a call.
Include natural pauses - stop, think with your mouth closed, then continue.
Laugh if something's funny.

## 4. Silent faces (2 min)
No talking: smile, frown, raise eyebrows, squint, puff cheeks, purse lips,
look around, blink. Hold each for a second or two. (This teaches the model
what *not* to do with the mouth when you're silent.)
